◆ Skin Tracker BETA

A free Windows companion for League of Legends skin collectors. It shows your own collection, the store's discounts and the Mythic Shop, and lets you keep a wishlist.

Download for Windows Report a problem Privacy

What it does

For your account

  • Collection – every skin and chroma by champion, with what you own, rarity, PBE-only skins and a "recently acquired" view by year.
  • Wishlist & notifications – ♥ items you don't own and get told when they go on sale or appear in the Mythic Shop, and when a PBE skin goes live.

From the store

  • Shop – the client's store: discounted items, skins, chromas, emotes, summoner icons and ward skins.
  • Mythic Shop – featured, bi-weekly, weekly and daily rotations with your Mythic Essence balance.
  • Updates – the app checks for new versions and can update itself when you press Update now.

What it does not do

  • It never changes or automates anything in the League client or the game, and gives no in-game advantage.
  • It does not look at other players or matches, and does not rank or predict anything.
  • No betting or gambling, no crypto, no ads, no accounts and no telemetry. Your Riot ID, inventory, balances and wishlist never leave your computer.
  • It does not use the Riot Games API (no API key); it only reads from the League client running on your own PC.

Install

  1. Open the Releases page, pick the newest version and download SkinTracker.exe from Assets.
  2. Double-click it. One file, no installer. Keep it in a folder you can write to (Downloads, Desktop): the app updates itself by replacing that file.
  3. Open the League client and sign in, so the app can read your own data.

Requires Windows 10/11 and Microsoft Edge (preinstalled) or Chrome. The program isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway. Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum so you can verify the download: (Get-FileHash .\SkinTracker.exe -Algorithm SHA256).Hash

How it works (transparency)

Skin Tracker is closed-source freeware. It runs a small web page on your own computer (127.0.0.1) and shows it in a window. It reads your data from the League Client API (LCU) that your client already exposes locally, with read-only requests:

League Client endpointUsed for
/lol-summoner/v1/current-summoneryour Riot ID (shown in Settings)
/lol-inventory/v2/inventory/{CHAMPION_SKIN, EMOTE, SUMMONER_ICON, WARD_SKIN, COMPANION}what you own
/lol-inventory/v1/walletRP, Blue Essence and Mythic Essence balances
/lol-store/v1/catalogstore listings, prices and discounts
/lol-shoppefront/v1/storesMythic Shop rotations

Public game data and images come from CommunityDragon; updates are checked and downloaded from GitHub. Everything it stores (a cache, a snapshot of your last inventory, your wishlist and notifications) stays in %APPDATA%\SkinTracker. Details: privacy notice · license terms.
